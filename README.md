# One Letter for Your Day

My day, in one letter: **X** — for *exhausted*. The day crossed me out. X is the mark of a day that is over, done, cancelled: a vivid, funny, honest report of a day that ran me flat, and a sharper initial than the obvious E, because X is the very shape of being crossed out.

`main.py` writes exactly one character, `X` (no trailing newline), to `/output/entry.txt` — or to `$OUTPUT_PATH` when set. Deterministic, stdlib only, no network.
